My projects

Treato

One shared health record for families, doctors and clinics in India. Families book doctors and keep their records together, doctors run their day from a phone, and clinics run the front desk from a desktop app. Here is what I built:

  • 4apps: patient, doctor, web, clinic desk
  • 3,700+commits since March 2025
  • ~360database migrations
  • 1engineer (hi, that's me)
  • Mobile. Patient and doctor apps in React Native and Expo, with booking, family records, in-app chat and LiveKit video visits.
  • Backend. The core API in Go (Fiber) on PostgreSQL: about 360 schema migrations, REST plus a read-only GraphQL layer, RabbitMQ between services.
  • Auth. A Rust service for phone OTP and passkeys, with one identity across patient, doctor and clinic roles.
  • AI. Maya, the assistant inside the patient app. It explains reports in plain words, keeps a health diary and drafts a brief before a visit. It never diagnoses.
  • Real time. Chat in Elixir and Phoenix, and self-hosted LiveKit for video.
  • Web and desktop. A Next.js website with a public doctor directory, and a Tauri app for clinic front desks (tokens, walk-ins, billing).
  • Infra. Docker on a self-hosted VPS with Dokploy, plus Elasticsearch and Dragonfly. I own the deploys and the backups.
